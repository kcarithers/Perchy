# Perchy

A flockmate for a lone budgie. Perchy shows an animated budgie on screen, listens for your bird's calls through the microphone, and answers with real wild budgie calls, picking ones close to your bird's pitch and favoring the calls your bird answers.

Open the GitHub Pages link on a phone or tablet by the cage, tap **Wake Perchy** and allow the microphone. Everything runs in the browser; nothing is uploaded.
