Replies are calm contact calls and chatter, with at least a few seconds between them. Alarm calls and squawks were left out. Perchy ignores its own sounds while listening, and can stay quiet overnight.

What Perchy is learning

Perchy answers with real wild budgie calls. It picks calls close to your bird's pitch and length, tunes them slightly toward your bird, and plays the calls your bird answers more often.
