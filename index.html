Replies stay soft and short, with at least a few seconds between them. Perchy never makes harsh or alarm-like calls, ignores its own sounds while listening, and can stay quiet overnight.

Perchy's dialect

Real flockmates slowly copy each other's calls. Perchy starts with its own voice and drifts toward the pitch, length and rhythm your bird uses most, without ever copying a call exactly.
